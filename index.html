About the Festival & Scope

Fostering education and research in quantum technologies in Colombia

The Quantum Experience

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is part of the global movement powered by IBM Quantum. Selected as an official local node, our university becomes a vibrant hub where students, researchers, and tech enthusiasts dive deep into the foundations and real-world applications of quantum computing.

Join us for four days of inspiring keynotes, interactive coding labs with Qiskit, and collaborative spaces designed to spark the next generation of quantum innovators in Colombia.
