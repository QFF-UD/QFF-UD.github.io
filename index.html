About the Festival & Scope

Fostering education and research in quantum technologies in Colombia

Academic Framework

The Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 at Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas is part of the global network of events supported by IBM Quantum to strengthen scientific and technological capabilities in quantum information.

Co-organized by the team of co-organizers and the SPSUMM research group (Simulation of Physical Systems Using Multiscale Methods), this in-person event brings together physics, mathematics, and engineering students.

Technical Requirements & FAQ

Key information for festival attendees

Recommended Python Environment

We recommend bringing your laptop for practical workshops. You can prepare your environment by running:

pip install qiskit qiskit-ibm-runtime matplotlib

Is registration free?

Yes, participation is 100% free with prior registration.

Are academic certificates provided?

Yes, a formal attendance certificate will be issued after the event.

Is advanced prior knowledge required?

Not mandatory. Week 1 covers introductory topics from scratch.
